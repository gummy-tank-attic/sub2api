# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last Updated:** October 6, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 6, 2026  

This Refund & Cancellation Policy (this "Policy") applies to all account balance top-ups, digital services, and paid plans on the **FXVIA** developer platform (the "Platform"), owned and operated by **HELIX TECH LLC** ("Company", "we", "us", or "our").

---

## 1. Nature of Services & Billing Model
FXVIA provides enterprise AI API gateway routing and digital infrastructure services. Because underlying compute resources and upstream model dispatches are consumed dynamically and in real time:
- **Consumed API Credits (Non-Refundable):** Any fees associated with API calls that have already been executed, tokens consumed, or compute allocations utilized represent immediately delivered digital computing resources and are **strictly non-refundable**.
- **Unconsumed Balance Refunds (Refundable):** Customers are entitled to request a full refund for any **unconsumed, valid prepaid balance** remaining in their account within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of the original deposit date. Deposits exceeding fourteen calendar days remain available in your account balance for ongoing use and are generally non-refundable.

## 2. Refund Procedure
To request a refund for an eligible, unconsumed prepaid balance, please follow these steps:
1. **Submit Request:** Send an email from your registered account email address to **contact@fixvia.com** with the subject line: `Refund Request - [Your Registered Email]`.
2. **Provide Details:** Include your top-up order number, payment transaction receipt, and the reason for your refund request.
3. **Review & Settlement:** Our support and billing department will verify your request within **three (3) business days**. Upon approval, the refund will be credited back to your original payment method. Settlement typically takes 5 to 10 business days, depending on your payment processor or issuing bank.

## 3. Account Cancellation & Termination
You may terminate your FXVIA account at any time directly through the dashboard or by contacting customer support. Upon account closure, if your account holds an eligible unconsumed prepaid balance pursuant to Section 1, you may request a refund prior to final account deletion.

## 4. Billing Inquiries & Dispute Resolution
If you have any questions regarding your statement or billing history, or believe there is a billing discrepancy, **please contact our official support team for verification and resolution prior to initiating a bank chargeback or dispute**. We are committed to resolving your concerns promptly and fairly:
- **Entity:** HELIX TECH LLC
- **Customer Support & Refund Email:** contact@fixvia.com
