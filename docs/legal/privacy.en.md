# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 6, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 6, 2026  

**HELIX TECH LLC** ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") owns and operates the **FXVIA** platform (the "Platform"). We deeply value your privacy rights and personal data security, and we strictly comply with globally recognized privacy protection standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

This Privacy Policy (this "Policy") details how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you access and use FXVIA services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide high-availability API gateway infrastructure and fulfill compliance auditing obligations, we collect the following necessary information:
- **Account Registration Information:** Email address, username, password hash, and necessary billing contact details provided during account registration.
- **Operational & Telemetry Data:** API request timestamps, model endpoints invoked, HTTP status codes, token usage counts, error logs, and visitor IP addresses required for anti-abuse protection and security auditing.
- **Payment Records:** Transaction reference identifiers and settlement confirmation records processed by certified third-party payment processors. We **never store** raw credit card numbers or sensitive payment card security codes (CVV) on our servers.
- **Zero Prompt Retention Policy:** All prompt payloads and model outputs routed and dispatched through the FXVIA gateway are processed strictly in-memory for streaming transmission; they are **never written to disk, never archived, and never used to train or fine-tune any AI models**.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the information we collect solely for the following legitimate purposes:
1. To operate, maintain, optimize, and secure the FXVIA API gateway infrastructure;
2. To accurately calculate and process usage-based billing and credit balance deductions;
3. To prevent fraud, abuse, DDoS attacks, and enforce our Acceptable Use Policy;
4. To fulfill statutory legal obligations, regulatory disclosures, and anti-money laundering (AML) compliance requirements.
We **never sell** your personal data, nor do we disclose your personal information to third-party data brokers.

## 3. Data Security & Protective Measures
We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data assets:
- End-to-end TLS 1.3 high-strength encryption for all data in transit across the platform;
- Strict internal access privilege separation based on the principle of least privilege;
- 24/7 automated anomalous traffic monitoring and tamper-evident audit logging.

## 4. Your Data Rights
Depending on your applicable jurisdiction's privacy laws, you may exercise the following statutory data rights:
- Access and obtain a copy of your personal data held by us;
- Correct or update inaccurate personal information;
- Request account closure and erasure of your personal data;
- Restrict or object to specific data processing activities.
To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request to the contact email listed in this Policy.
